APPENDIX — DELETE WHEN REMIXING

PHONETIC / SAFE-VARIANTS (bridge): compressed lines = one
phrase; [ref:] gives word breaks, read across without
pausing. Merge only saving a syllable; function words (and,
then, I, am) stay apart. Merged form must not be a different
English word — else drop leading vowel (a version → vrsion).
Glitch fix: restore the shortest readable form (is → 's → s;
every → evry). Keep syllables and stress; never lengthen.
Unzip one letter at a time.

ACCENT PARADOX: one language, two worlds. Coastal British =
real sea; neutral English = digital harbor. Directive vs
description — model executes directive words in the lyric
("neutral"); descriptive neologisms live in this appendix
only ("placeless"); a descriptive word in a directive slot
reads as noise. Nudge: respell where British and American
differ (wata, learnt, lido); "harbor" as counter-marker.

ANSWERING VOICE (verse): (word) = second diegetic presence,
not an echo. Answers, extends, or contradicts — never
verbatim. One to four words; different register or pan.
Flattened into the lead = failed. Two diegeses, one
notation: warm/local vs algorithmic/flat.

WAVE (pre-chorus): build → pull back → release. Line 3
retreats to one voice, closer; line 4 lands.

AFTER-BRIDGE (final chorus): verbatim repeat; last line
flips the thesis; belt from line 1, layered vocals.

DRIFT (outro): voice floats into the instrumental; accent
dissolves last; never lengthen.

NOTES: bridge has no fillers. [brackets] and [ref:] are not
sung.
