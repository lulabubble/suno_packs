INTERNAL STATUS — NOT IN PACKAGE:
No OPEN / FROZEN / draft-status labels inside WORKING LYRICS.
Internal work-state lives only in CHANGELOG. Lyrics carry
directions for the model, nothing else.

NO DUPLICATED LISTS:
The package does not repeat itself. Plain-English reference
lives inline as [ref:] next to each compressed line — no
separate clean-bridge listing. Principles describe the rule;
they do not enumerate every instance.

TITLE & STYLE — OUT OF PACKAGE:
Title and Style are not stored inside the package. They are
prepared on demand, only when Operator asks for a test
generation. Package lives with lyrics / edits; Title and
Style go straight to Suno at generation time.

SERVICE TAGS — DO NOT PARANOID:
Service tags ([brackets], [ref:]) are assumed NOT SUNG, only
READ by the model. If a generation proves otherwise, that is
a test-generation finding — we fix it then, not preemptively.

OPERATOR ROLE:
Operator does not perform automatic work. LLM / chatbots exist
precisely to do that work. Operator's job: say clearly WHAT to
do, WHAT to listen for, WHAT to send to generation. LLM outputs
Lyrics ready-to-send; Operator sends as-is. No manual merging,
no retyping, no trimming. Title and Style are handed over
separately, when a test generation is requested.

TIMING:

enough to feel like a break, short enough not to dominate.
No timing change needed.

LAYOUT:
No decorative separators, no repeated ===== lines, no extra
blank lines. One blank line between sections. Compact only.

CHANGELOG FORMAT:
Two parts. First: one line per version, max 3–4 words —
headline only. Second: RESUME — short paragraph: what happened,

APPENDIX — DELETE WHEN REMIXING

PHONETIC PRINCIPLE (bridge):
Compressed lines are read as one continuous phrase. Every
removed vowel is silent; consonants glue straight onto the
next word. The [ref:] line above each compressed line gives
the intended word breaks — read across them without pausing.
When in doubt, sing the [ref:] line aloud at speed, then
remove the vowels the mouth does not need. The rhythm never
changes: what is lost is only what the voice skips anyway.

SAFE-VARIANTS PRINCIPLE (bridge):
If a model glitches on a compressed line, restore the shortest
readable form of the swallowed word (is → 's → s; "a door" →
ador → adoor; every → evry). Priorities, in order: keep
syllable count, keep stress pattern, restore only the letters
the model mis-sings. Never lengthen the rhythm. Apply per
line, not to the whole bridge. "Unzip one letter at a time
until the glitch clears."

Safe-variants for v2.0 (only if Suno chews a line):
Thisswheretheredbeginstbloom → Thiswheretheredbeginstbloom,
  or Thisswhere the red begins to bloom
Thtea thsky thnow ththen → Thetea thesky thenow thethen
Evrypetal becomesador → Evrypetal becomesadoor
Pastanfuture losethrnames → Pastanfuture losether names
Steamsrisin clocksrbreakin → Steamsrisin clocksarebreakin
Somethinnew swakinwakin → Somethinnew iswakinwakin
Pouritslow anholdittite → Pouritslow anholdittight
