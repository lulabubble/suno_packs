APPENDIX — DELETE WHEN REMIXING

PHONETIC / SAFE-VARIANTS (bridge): compressed lines = one
phrase; [ref:] gives word breaks, read across without
pausing. Merge only saving a syllable; function words (and,
then, I, am) stay apart. Merged form must not be a different
English word — else drop leading vowel (a version → vrsion).
Glitch fix: restore the shortest readable form (is → 's → s;
every → evry). Keep syllables and stress; never lengthen.
Unzip one letter at a time.

ANSWERING VOICE (verse): (word) = second diegetic presence,
not an echo. Answers, extends, or contradicts — never
verbatim. One to four words; different register or pan. In
V1 the voice is the therapist — cold, algorithmic, flat,
right-panned. It does not warm.

VOICE INHERITANCE (V2 → PC2): the answering voice
disappears at the end of V2. From that point the narrator
speaks in the therapist's register — cold, flat, algorithmic
— because the narrator has become the prescriber. Same tone,
new mouth. The voice does not return.

DIEGETIC SILENCE (V2 end): [silence — answering voice gone]
is a direction, not a lyric. The pause where the therapist
would have answered is the last appearance of the therapist.

VOICE STACKING (pre-chorus): build by adding voices, not
volume. Line 1 = one voice; each next adds distinct voices
(register, pan, echo). Last line = full unison. Never double
the lead verbatim. In PC1 the stack stays cold and
algorithmic — same actor layered. In PC2 the stack keeps
that register after it has been inherited.

CHORUS REWRITE (chorus 2): chorus 2 repeats the first two
lines verbatim; the last two lines are rewritten. The
narrator takes the prescription into his own hands: "the
pill is a song" becomes "I swallow the chorus"; "I take it"
becomes "I make it". Delivery unchanged — the change is in
the text alone.

COLLAPSE (final chorus): FC belts from the first line as
usual, then drops — drums cut, synth drone only — and the
thesis returns spoken, quiet, cold, under the drone. Peak
does not resolve; it collapses into the thesis. Never
lengthen the drop; silence inside the drone is the point.

UNDERSTATEMENT (outro): outro does not conclude — it
decays. Voice stops finishing words, lines, thoughts.
Silence becomes content. Cut where the voice would run out.
Never lengthen. Returning fragments return smaller. Read as
continuous decay.

NOTES: bridge has no fillers. [brackets] and [ref:] are not
sung.
