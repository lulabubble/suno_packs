APPENDIX — DELETE WHEN REMIXING

PHONETIC / SAFE-VARIANTS: compressed lines = one phrase;
[ref:] gives word breaks, read across without pausing. Merge
only to save a syllable; function words (and, then, I, am)
stay apart. Merged form must not be a different English word
— else drop leading vowel (a version → vrsion). Glitch fix:
restore shortest readable form (is → 's → s; every → evry).
Keep syllables and stress; never lengthen. Unzip one letter
at a time.

ANSWERING VOICE (verse): (word) = second presence, not echo.
Answers, extends, or contradicts — never verbatim. One to
four words; different register/pan. In V1 it is the
therapist: cold, algorithmic, flat, right-panned. Does not
warm.

VOICE INHERITANCE / DIEGETIC SILENCE (V2 end): the voice
disappears. [silence — answering voice gone] is a direction,
not a lyric; the pause is the therapist's last appearance.
From then the narrator speaks in the therapist's register —
cold, flat, algorithmic — now the prescriber. Same tone, new
mouth. No return.

VOICE STACKING (pre-chorus): build by adding voices, not
volume. Line 1 = one voice; each next adds distinct voices
(register, pan, echo). Last line = full unison. Never double
lead verbatim. PC1 = same actor layered, in therapist's
register. PC2 keeps it after inheritance.

CHORUS REWRITE (chorus 2): first two lines verbatim; last
two rewritten. Narrator takes the prescription: pill = song
→ I swallow the chorus; I take it → I make it. Delivery
unchanged — change is in text alone.

COLLAPSE (FC): belts from line 1, then drops — drums cut,
drone only — thesis returns spoken, quiet, cold. Peak does
not resolve; collapses into the thesis. Never lengthen the
drop; silence inside the drone is the point.

UNDERSTATEMENT (outro): outro does not conclude — it decays.
Voice stops finishing words, lines, thoughts. Silence
becomes content. Cut where the voice would run out. Never
lengthen. Fragments shrink. Read as decay. Bridge has no
fillers; [brackets] and [ref:] not sung.
